The programs, in detail

Four programs of 8 to 12 weeks, played live in every session.

Program 01 · The whole foundation

Listening & yes-and

Yes-and is quoted constantly and practiced rarely. We work on listening without planning your next line, accepting offers as given, and adding something real — plus recognizing the polite blocks that look like agreement and quietly kill scenes.

  • 10 weeks
  • Complete beginners
  • 2 hours a week
  • A webcam and space to stand

Program 02 · Relationships over jokes

Scene work

Scenes work when the audience knows who these two people are to each other. We establish relationship early, find what's unusual and play it again, and heighten in a way that stays grounded — because a scene that goes crazy in thirty seconds has nowhere left to go.

  • 12 weeks
  • Some experience
  • 3 hours a week
  • Willingness to look foolish

Start this program

Program 03 · Beyond funny voices

Character & physicality

Most beginner characters are an accent and nothing else. We build them from body, pace and point of view, work on object work that convinces the audience a space exists, and play with status — which changes a scene faster than any new idea.

  • 8 weeks
  • Comfortable in scenes
  • 3 hours a week
  • Room to move

Start this program

Weeks 1–2 · Body first

Weeks 3–4 · Point of view

Weeks 1–2 · Nerves

Weeks 7–8 · A show

Prepared, performed, debriefed.

Program 04 · In front of people

Performing

Playing for an audience is a different skill. We work on nerves and what to do with them, on supporting rather than starring, on group forms and initiations, and on the simple fact that audiences enjoy watching people have fun far more than watching people be clever.

  • 8 weeks
  • Scene work solid
  • 3 hours a week
  • A group to play with
